# Deep Work — Cal Newport

## Key ideas

- shallow work is easy to replicate, deep work compounds
- schedule every minute of the day, then revise the schedule freely
- embrace boredom: attention is a muscle, not a switch
- quit social media as an experiment, re-add only what earns its place

## My takeaways

- morning writing block before opening any inbox
- phone stays in the hallway drawer during focus sessions
- a shutdown ritual at 6pm ends the workday cleanly
