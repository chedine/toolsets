# Carbonara

## Ingredients

- spaghetti 400g
- guanciale 150g, cut into small strips
- 4 egg yolks plus 1 whole egg
- pecorino romano, lots, finely grated
- black pepper, coarsely ground

## Method

- render the guanciale slowly, no oil needed
- whisk eggs and pecorino into a paste
- combine off the heat with a splash of pasta water
- never let the eggs scramble — residual heat only
